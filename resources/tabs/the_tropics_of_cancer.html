Currituck Co. - The Tropics of Cancer

Tuning: Open D (DADF#AD)
Capo: +4

This is a simple open-tuning piece from Kevin Barker’s solo project Currituck Co. It appears on Ghost Man on First, his second full album under that name, released in 2003, although many people (including me) are more familiar with it from its appearance on the 2004 compilation The Golden Apples of the Sun, put together by Devendra Barnhart.

If you’re a Fahey acolyte, this sort of composition should be familiar. The piece is played in a languid style, with the opening partial strum of the first notes in each bar in the A section drawn out by a bit. There are three basic sections. The A section is played first, leading into the B section, which launches back into the A section in the midst of the third repeat. The second A section swaps bars 1 and 2. The B section is then repeated again, with the main four measures repeated four times, before the piece moves into the C section. Finally, the C section dovetails back with the first four measures to conclude.
